ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Helping students find a place to belong.

Our goal is to make the accommodation search clearer and easier.

PROJECT BACKGROUND

Why StudentStay SL?

Finding suitable student accommodation can involve searching through different sources, comparing prices and contacting property owners individually.

StudentStay SL is a proposed website concept designed to organise accommodation information in one accessible place for students in Sierra Leone, with an initial focus on students around Limkokwing University.

The Problem

Students may find it difficult to compare room types, understand accommodation facilities and identify options that fit their budgets.

Our Proposed Solution

The website presents accommodation listings with photos, locations, sample prices and room information. Students can browse options and make enquiries.

WHO WE SERVE

Our target users

New Students

Students who need to explore places to stay before or after arriving on campus.

Returning Students

Students who want to compare alternative accommodation options.

Property Owners

Owners who may wish to advertise genuine accommodation to students in the future.
